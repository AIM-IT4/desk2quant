- *Hedging under model misspecification: All risk factors are equal, but some are more equal than others* (Journal of Futures Markets, 2012): https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/fut.20530
- *Hedging Valuation Adjustment and Model Risk* (arXiv): https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.11834
