actual catalog, the Complete Front Office & Risk Quant Professional Bundle,
the live session cards, and the booking form. The simulator does not appear.

- Light graph paper, heavy black rules, dark navy, and teal reflect the live site.
- Screenshots captured from the public site on 26 September 2026. Visible prices
