Refund Policy

Eligibility, timeframes, and the process for refunding mentorship session bookings and digital resource purchases.

Last updated: February 2026 Response time: 24–48 hours

1 Mentorship Sessions

  • Full Refund: Available if cancellation is made 24+ hours before scheduled session
  • Partial Refund: 50% refund for cancellations 12–24 hours before session
  • No Refund: For cancellations less than 12 hours or no-shows
  • Rescheduling: Free rescheduling available up to 24 hours before session

2 Digital Products

  • Non-Downloaded Products: Full refund within 7 days if not downloaded
  • Downloaded Products: No refund once download link is accessed
  • Defective Files: Full refund or replacement if files are corrupted or inaccessible
  • Preview Products: Free Test Product (₹0) is non-refundable

3 How to Request a Refund

To request a refund:

  • Send an email to jha.8@alumni.iitj.ac.in
  • Include your order/payment ID
  • State the reason for refund request
  • Allow 3–5 business days for processing

4 Processing Time

  • Approved refunds processed within 5–7 business days
  • Refunds issued to original payment method
  • Bank processing times may vary (additional 3–5 days)
  • You will receive email confirmation once processed

5 Exceptions

Refunds may not be granted in the following cases:

  • Change of mind after session completion
  • Customer's inability to attend due to technical issues on their end
  • Violation of Terms of Service
  • Bulk purchase orders (special terms apply)

6 Contact for Refunds

For refund requests or questions about this policy:

jha.8@alumni.iitj.ac.in Response within 24–48 hours

We strive to handle all refund requests fairly and promptly. For any disputes, please contact us directly.
