Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data Desk2Quant collects, how it's used, and the rights you have over it when you book a session or purchase a digital product.

1 Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name and email address for booking sessions
  • Payment information processed securely through Razorpay
  • Session preferences and communication history
  • Usage data to improve our services

2 How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and manage mentorship sessions
  • To process payments and send receipts
  • To send session reminders and follow-up materials
  • To improve our services and user experience
  • To comply with legal obligations

3 Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We share only what's necessary with the service providers that power Desk2Quant:

  • Razorpay — to process payments (handles your card/UPI details directly; we never store full payment credentials)
  • Supabase — to securely store booking, purchase, and account records
  • Our email service provider — to send booking confirmations, receipts, and requested resources

We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

4 Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information:

  • All payments processed through PCI-compliant Razorpay
  • Data stored in secure Supabase infrastructure
  • Email communications via secure email service
  • Regular security audits and updates

5 Data Retention

We retain your booking, purchase, and communication records for as long as needed to provide our services, comply with tax and accounting obligations, and resolve any disputes. You may request deletion of your data at any time, subject to records we're legally required to keep (such as payment records).

6 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
