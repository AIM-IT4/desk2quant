About this resource

Credit is the hardest asset class to model correctly — and the easiest to misunderstand. Most candidates learn intensity models, copulas, or structural models in isolation. What they are never taught is why credit behaves fundamentally differently from equity, FX, or rates, why models fail precisely when they matter most, and why Jump-to-Default (JTD) dominates credit PnL.

This guide is a desk-first, decision-driven playbook for credit modeling, written to bridge the gap between textbook theory and real trading, risk, and interview expectations. This is not an academic treatment of credit models. It is how credit desks actually price, hedge, explain losses, and survive crises.
