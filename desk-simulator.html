Questions

Before you enter the desk.

Is this a trading system or model certification tool?+

No. It is an educational simulation built from fictional cases. It does not connect to markets, execute trades, certify models or replace independent professional review.

Do I need an account or payment method?+

No. The launch beta is immediately usable without an account or card. Completed scores are saved in your browser on this device.

Is there one correct investigation path?+

No. You may run any available test. Scoring rewards decisive evidence and penalises only inefficient or unsafe choices; the debrief explains the causal chain.
